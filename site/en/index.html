Small tools,

Swiss Knife gathers 12 tools for the page you are looking at into a small window you move around the page, or into the side panel. Screenshots, colors, QR codes, contrast checks, test forms and more, without ever switching tabs.

The catalog

Twelve tools, one panel

You can show, hide and reorder the tools from the settings, so the panel holds only the ones you actually use.

Screenshots

Full page, viewport or a selected rectangle. PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Colors

Eyedropper, palettes pulled from the page, HEX, RGB, OKLCH and Tailwind class.

QR codes

URL, Wi‑Fi, vCard and more. Custom shapes, colors and logo, as PNG or SVG.

Contrast

Contrast ratio plus AA and AAA results for the WCAG 2.1 criteria.

Inspect and save

Element properties, HTML and CSS snippet, PNG preview.

Media capture

Images, video, audio and subtitles inside the element you pick.

Form filler

Coherent fake data, in Italian or English, without submitting the form.

Emoji

Search in Italian and English, skin tone, click to copy or drag.

Encode and convert

UTF‑8, Base64, URL, HEX, binary. MD5, SHA‑256, SHA‑512 and SM3 hashes.

Password generator

From 4 to 64 characters, generated with crypto.getRandomValues().

Lorem Ipsum

From 1 to 100 paragraphs of placeholder text, ready to copy.

Iframe list

Embedded content, nested included, opened in a new tab.

Privacy

It collects nothing

Runs locally

Conversions, hashes, passwords and page analysis all happen in the browser. Results are never sent anywhere.

No account

There is no sign-up, and no developer-operated server to contact.

Permissions on request

Access to all sites is optional, is never granted at install time, and can be revoked at any moment.
